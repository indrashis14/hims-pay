Late payment rules

Pay on or before the last date and you pay only the fee. After that a late fine is added:

  • ₹5 per day for days 1 to 10 after the last date
  • ₹10 per day for days 11 to 20
  • After 20 days, online payment closes. Contact the HIMS accounts office.
